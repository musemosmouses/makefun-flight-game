SKYHOOK VALLEY

Bush-plane ring run. Take off from the dirt strip, thread 12 glowing rings across the valley — low over the lake, through the canyon, around the peak — then land back on the runway before your fuel or your time runs out.

W / S throttle  ·  ↑ ↓ pitch  ·  ← → / A D bank & turn
Q / E rudder  ·  F flaps  ·  B brakes  ·  C cockpit cam
P pause  ·  R restart  ·  M mute  ·  drag the mouse to steer

Each ring: +100 pts and +6 s. Landing: +250 pts, plus time and fuel bonuses. Refuel only by landing. Watch the stall — below 47 kt the wing quits.
